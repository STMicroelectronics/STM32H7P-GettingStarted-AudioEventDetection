Update History

Alpha release

DFP alpha release supporting STM32H7P4xx and STM32H7P5xx

  • CMSIS Device Header & SVD updates
    • ADC, ADF, COMP, CRC, DAC, DBGMCU, DMA, DLYB, DTS, FDCAN, FMC,
    • GPDMA, GPIO, HASH, HPDMA, HRTIM, HSP, I2C, I3C, IWDG, LPTIM, NPU
    • RAMECC, RCC, RNG, SBS, SPI, TAMP, TIM; UCPD, U[S]ART/LPUART, USB OTG FS, VREFBUF, XSPI
  • Add peripheral_configuration_mapping.json
  • Add new low level cortex APIs

Known Limitations

  • STM32H7P Device Family Pack is currently in beta version and may be subject to future fixes and updates.

  • None.
